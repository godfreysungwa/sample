Terms & Conditions

1. Website Purpose & Order Inquiries

This website serves as a product catalog and inquiry platform for Nyirefami Limited. Submitting items through the Order Cart generates a WhatsApp order inquiry and does not constitute a binding contract or final purchase until our sales team confirms product availability, current pricing, and delivery arrangements.

2. Pricing & Payment Confirmation

Prices for retail and wholesale cereal flour packages are quoted directly via WhatsApp, phone, or official invoice upon receiving your inquiry. Payment terms (such as mobile money, bank transfer, or cash on delivery within Arusha) are agreed upon prior to order dispatch.

3. Delivery & Regional Dispatch

Local deliveries within Arusha and upcountry bus/cargo dispatches across Tanzania are scheduled once payment or order confirmation is completed. Delivery timelines and transport charges depend on destination and order volume.

4. Product Quality, Storage & Claims

All Nyirefami cereal flours are milled, cleaned, and packaged under strict hygiene standards. Customers should inspect packages upon receipt and store flours in a cool, dry place. Any damaged packaging or quality concern must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos and batch details.

5. Customer Privacy & Data Use

Information entered in the Order Cart form (your name, phone number, and delivery location) is stored locally on your device during browsing and sent directly to Nyirefami Limited via WhatsApp solely to process your inquiry and coordinate delivery. We do not sell or share customer contact details with third parties.
